About RRR-Galaxy.pk

RRR-Galaxy.pk is an online marketplace built around one simple idea: everyday life should feel easier, more comfortable and more efficient.

We bring together beauty and personal-care products, useful electronic gadgets, smart devices and home-use products. Each one is chosen to be practical and affordable, from a rechargeable beauty device to a kitchen helper that saves you ten minutes.

We serve shoppers across Pakistan, with Cash on Delivery and easy ordering through our website or WhatsApp. If you ever have a question, our team is happy to help.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about ordering, delivery and support.

Do you offer Cash on Delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery is our main payment option. You pay when your order is delivered. Availability can depend on your area, and we confirm this when we contact you about your order.

Where do you deliver?

We aim to deliver across Pakistan. Delivery to some remote areas may take longer or may not be possible, so we confirm your location when we verify your order.

How can I place an order?

Add products to your cart, press Checkout, fill in your delivery details and place the order. You can also send the same order summary to us on WhatsApp.

How can I contact customer support?

Message us on WhatsApp, send an email, or use the contact form on this website. Our contact details are in the Contact section.

Can I order through WhatsApp?

Yes. After adding products to your cart, choose Order on WhatsApp. Your products, quantities, total price, address and phone number are filled into the message automatically.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your city and on product availability. We confirm the expected delivery time when we confirm your order.

Can I return a product?

Please check our current return policy before ordering. If you have a question about a specific product or order, contact us and we will help.
